How to Start a Website in Kenya: A Beginner's Guide

A practical introduction to choosing a purpose, registering a domain, and arranging website hosting.

1. Decide what your website is for

Start with one clear purpose. You might want to publish articles, display your services, create a portfolio, or introduce an online business. Knowing your goal helps you choose the right structure and content.

2. Choose a domain name

A domain is the address people type to visit your website. Choose something short, easy to spell, and relevant to your topic. Before registering it, check the price, renewal cost, registration period, and whether the domain ending you want is available.

3. Understand website hosting

Hosting is a service that stores the files and data that make a website available online. Some simple websites can use free static hosting, while sites that need databases, email, content management systems, or server-side features may need a paid hosting plan.

4. Compare providers before paying

Compare the full price, renewal fees, storage and bandwidth limits, support options, backup arrangements, security features, and refund terms. Read the provider's current terms and make sure the plan supports the kind of website you intend to build. Do not buy a plan just because an affiliate recommends it.

5. Publish useful, original content

Start with a clear homepage and a few helpful pages or articles. Explain things accurately, keep information updated, make the site easy to use on phones, and provide a way for visitors to contact you where appropriate.
